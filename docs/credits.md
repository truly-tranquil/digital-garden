the garden radio has no tracks and no files: the music is generated live by your browser (web audio), it belongs to nobody, and it exists only while you listen. there is nothing to attribute and nothing to license there.

## icons

the small icons used across the garden — note glyphs, interface icons, and the trinkets hidden in notes — come from these sets:

- [Phosphor Icons](https://phosphoricons.com) by Helena Zhang — [MIT](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/homepage/blob/main/LICENSE)
- [Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons) by Paweł Kuna — [MIT](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/LICENSE)
- [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) — [ISC](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE)
- [Lucide Lab](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/lab) — [ISC](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE)
- [Material Design Icons](https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/) by Google / Pictogrammers — [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign-SVG/blob/master/LICENSE)
- [IconOven](https://iconify.design/icon-sets/iconoven/) by the IconOven contributors — MIT *(reserve shelf, not yet in the sprite)*
- [@icons](https://iconify.design/icon-sets/icons/) by Mikobrown — MIT *(reserve shelf, not yet in the sprite)*
- [Newsreader](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Newsreader) by Production Type — [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://github.com/productiontype/Newsreader/blob/main/OFL.txt)

any icon above can hide in a note as a collectible (`<!-- trinket:name -->`); the shelf only shows what the site uses at build time. sources and licenses for every glyph in the list: [the icons page](/for-my-reference/icons.html).
