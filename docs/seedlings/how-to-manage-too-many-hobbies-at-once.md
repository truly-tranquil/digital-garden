1. Make a list of everything in progress/ that you want to start now
2. come up with a reasonable goal or benchmark for each
	1. eg. 1 chapter, 10 minutes, 1 row, etc.
3. work on each thing in a rotational cycle (this is not a requirement, just what makes it easier for me)
	1. eg. mon i read a chapter out of my 1st book and do a row of knitting and then spend 15 minutes researching something im interested in, wednes i watch an episode of an ongoing tv show read a chapter out of my second book and try a new recipe for dinner, etc.
4. make a list of all the future things you want to do
5. give yourself a milestone to achieve before you start
	1. eg. i can start a new fiction book when i finish the last, i can start learning how to knit after i finish this diamond painting, etc.
