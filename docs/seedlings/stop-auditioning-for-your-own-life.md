# stop auditioning for your own life

you already know, deep down, what it is that you are meant to do. stop trying to earn the right to be where you are, and instead be present with yourself. you have no worth to earn, you are born with it intrinsic to your being. you have no place of belonging you need to strive for, the people who matter saved your seat already. why are you waiting to be chosen. choose yourself. rejection is not failure.
