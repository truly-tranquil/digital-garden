#### Break The Sub-Goals Into Specific Questions (i once again use wikipedia to find these because i still usually have no clue wtf im talking about)
-  how to draw
	 - materials of the craft
		 - what do i need to get started?
		 - what do the professionals use?
		 - what are the materials specifically used for?
	 - anatomy
		 - what is the difference between human/animal/plant anatomy?
		 - is it best to draw anatomy still or in motion?
	 - still life
		 - how to draw an apple? (too specific)
		 - what is a still life?
		 - are still lives the key to unlocking my true potential (probably too vast a question to tackle all at once but you do you)
	 - lighting and shading
		 - where does light come from and how do i show that?
		 - what are the different shading techniques?
		 - etc.
