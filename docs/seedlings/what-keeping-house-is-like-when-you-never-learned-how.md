# what keeping house is like when you never learned how

i should say clarify that i was taught the basics; how to put laundry into the wash, how to run a dishwasher, how to vacuum. what i didn't learn is how often you should change your linens, is dusting really necessary, and i definitely missed a lesson in how to create a welcoming environment free of mess and decay.

today i will explain how i learned these things on my own in my mid-twenties and why it really is important for everyone to know, not just stay-at-homes.
