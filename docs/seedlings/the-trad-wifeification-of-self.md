# the trad wifeification of self

yeah so i, an adult man, feel the draw towards being a house spouse more with every day. it's not out of desire to not work, or even to serve anyone else, but out of a pure desire to take care of my home, my self, and my loved ones. does anyone else feel this way? i imagine as the world grows more hostile, something in my brain chemistry adapted to want my home to be the safest and most comforting it could be. a shelter from the storm if you will. i think having a place like that is a need that not nearly enough people have (as i believe everyone should), and it can affect anyone even the housed.
