hello, and welcome to the about the author page. i am a mid-twenties man who is inspired by learning and creativity in every aspect of his day-to-day life.

right now, i think my biggest passions are fiber arts, the concept of learning how to learn, and this digital garden.

i discovered what it meant to truly have your own little corner of the internet, and incorporating that with my love of learning and keeping track of everything called to me in a way that not much else ever has.

i have a very involved journaling system that i've been keeping for years. applying that to a digital space — where i can share that knowledge and information and love of learning, and also keep it organized in one central location, with other people — felt like the next natural step.

currently i am working to finish up the visual styling of this site as well as just adding any final features and touches and things like that before i fully send into writing and creating and drawing and whatever else i see fit to add to my notes here.

but i think that most importantly i am working on becoming comfortable with the idea that people will see what i really think and not just what i allow them to see.

if you would like to get in contact with me, you are more than welcome to. i will have a link to my link tree [right here](https://linktr.ee/truly.tranquil){ data-icon="profile" } and i am pretty active on my socials, so i respond usually within a day or two. so feel free to contact me that way.

otherwise, there's also a forum page that you can find at the bottom of the index and also in the footer of every page on the site that leads to my github discussions page, and you can contact me and other people in the community who see my work and talk with them there.

i love hearing from people, so it really does make my day. thanks for sticking around. if you've read this whole thing, you're really cool.
