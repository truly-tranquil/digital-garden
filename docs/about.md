# About this digital garden

This repository is a personal garden of notes that grow through reading, learning, making, and revision. It is not a blog and not a wiki: notes are thoughts that may wander, loop back on themselves, and change shape over time.

## How to read this garden

The garden is organized into three stages that describe how settled a note is:

- **Seedlings** — new or developing thoughts. These are the ideas still being tested and watered. Some may sprout into something more substantial; others may not survive.
- **Buds** — thoughts that have taken shape and are being tended. They are close to what you would call done, but may still need a season of revision.
- **Evergreens** — thoughts that have stood the test of time. They are stable, reviewed, and likely to remain relevant across seasons.

Open the stage you are interested in, or use the [by-tag](/by-tag/) index to find notes by theme.

## Navigating the garden

- The **home page** (`docs/index.md`) is a landing page, not a content page.
- The **manifesto** (`docs/manifesto.md`) explains the purpose and principles behind the notes.
- The **reference page** (`docs/for-my-reference/reference-page.md`) documents the front matter, links, embeds, and formatting conventions used in this garden.
- Use the **search** feature to find notes by keyword.

## Contributing

If you want to add a note or change how the garden works, read [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](../.github/CODEOWNERS) file. Governance is a work in progress; the maintainers are listed in the CODEOWNERS file and can be added to over time.

## A note on tone

This garden contains personal reflections, opinions, and speculations. It is not a verified source of factual information. We welcome respectful disagreement and discussion in the giscus comments on most pages.
