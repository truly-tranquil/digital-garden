Manifesto¶

why this garden exists¶

i honestly saw the idea of a digital garden and noticed i had a similar sort of thing going on with my journal system at the time. i switched to notion and then obsidian and finally landed here. my own custom site where i can be authentically myself, learn in public, and grow alongside my thoughts.

what belongs here¶

everything i have ever thought about twice. that is my general rule. if i thought about it more than once it is ingrained in my head and i should nurture it like i would a living plant.

how notes grow¶

notes are thoughts. thoughts are ever evolving. if i think it twice i plant it. if i come across new information i fertilize. if the thought no longer serves it's purpose where it is, it get's composted to help make room for other ideas. it's all a metaphor but it is also quite literally how i garden in real life.

how I learn and create¶

i really just let my everything flow free. if i have a whim, i follow it. if i have an inclination, i investigate it. genuinely i just allow my brain to do the talking and my hands just follow suit.

how I tend ideas over time¶

similar in concept to [#how notes grow], i have a random button on my end too! it takes me to a random note and i re-read it and make changes as feel fitting. plus if it's a seedling i go through and do some research to add sources and images.

what this garden is not¶

this is not a guaranteed source of factual information. while i do my best to avoid verifiably false information, i also have opinions, speculations, and some times just fuck up. this is also not a space for hatred. politics is one thing, and i absolutely encourage it both in my own investigations into my mind, but also people's responses and critiques of my writing. that being said if your idea of politics is wether or not someone deserves basic human rights (see: food, water, shelter, life, liberty, and the ability to pursue happiness for themselves), then you and i have nothing to talk about. i do not accept arguments in which anyone (and i do mean anyone) is to be treated less for simply existing as they are.

an invitation¶

if you've made it this far, first of all thank you for taking the time to read this! i really do pour my heart into this site, and it means the world to me that my words are reaching other people. that being said, do you disagree with everything i have ever said? maybe reflect on that a little. but do you genuinely have a question, comment, or concern about something i've said? then please reach out! here is a link to my linktree. and i also have giscus discussions embedded on most pages.
